# Heraldia

**English** · [Español](README.es.md) · [Français](README.fr.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Italiano](README.it.md) · [Português (Brasil)](README.pt-BR.md) · [Català](README.ca.md) · [Galego](README.gl.md)

A Fabric mod for **Minecraft 26.3** that fills the Overworld with places worth the journey: living villages joined by roads, ruins with a story, guardians with a name and treasure at the end of the map. Everything is built from vanilla blocks and items, so it fits right into your world.

## What you will find in your world

- 🏘️ **Living villages.** 72 hand-designed villages: 36 styles, one for each kind of biome (a Castilian village on the plains, stilt houses in the swamp, a Japanese village among the cherry trees...), each in a standard and a large size, grouped in 12 families. A square with a fountain and a bell, a church, a market, a tavern, a smithy, a library, an inn and twenty or thirty houses. Each style has its own trades, the bailiff's job board pays for harvests, firewood and clearing the roads, and the cartographers sell treasure maps. Here and there, someone was just working: a house half built with its scaffolding, laundry hanging out, a cart being unloaded, a cooking fire with the pot on.
- 🛤️ **Roads.** Trails with lamp posts and wooden bridges join neighbouring villages. Near each village a sign tells you the name of the next one and how many blocks are left.
- 🏕️ **By the wayside.** 16 kinds of medium structures between villages: watchtowers, roadside inns, bandit camps, hermitages, mills, fisher's huts, hunters' lodges, ruined shrines and wizard's huts, each in the style of its biome, with modest loot and now and then a small challenge. Beyond the portals, the Nether has renegade piglin outposts, magma altars, basalt watchtowers and fungus shrines, and the outer End islands have void observatories, purpur mausoleums and fallen shulker wharves.
- 🏰 **21 unique structures**, very rare and each in its own biome: a castle with a moat, a Gothic cathedral, a dwarven citadel carved into the mountain, a stranded galleon, a haunted mansion, an eastern palace, a wizard's tower, a lost mine deep underground, a pirate fort on its own island in open sea... Inside: hidden rooms, redstone puzzles, traps and spawners, and at the end, a **named guardian** guarding the treasure chamber. Two of them lie beyond the portals: the Ashen Citadel in the Nether and the Sanctum of the Void on the outer End islands, far from the dragon.
- ⚔️ **Guardians that fight in phases.** As they lose health they become enraged and call reinforcements from their own structure, with a boss bar while the fight lasts. Each one drops its own trophy.
- ⛏️ **Mines.** On real hillsides open adits, mining camps and mining complexes with a headframe, in 3 sizes and in the style of each biome, leading down to galleries with rails, crossings and rooms. Vanilla abandoned mineshafts are also more common.
- 🗼 **Lighthouses and wrecks.** Lighthouses on firm ground by the sea, with their light always lit, the keeper's house and a jetty, and broken galleons run aground on the beach or half-sunk in the shallows, with their hold and the captain's cabin. Each in the style of its coast: temperate, warm, cold or snowy.
- 🔱 **Atlantis.** Only one per world: a vast city sunk in the deep ocean, with dry halls, three seals with their keys and the Guardian of Poseidon, keeper of the greatest treasure in Heraldia.
- 🏟️ **Wave arena.** A coliseum near spawn: ring the bell and hold out against ever stronger waves, with a champion every fifth wave, records and rewards. If you fall in the ring, you keep your inventory. Three modes (co-op, time attack and survival) with a weekly leaderboard, `/heraldia arena leaderboard`: last week's champions go up on the signs of the arena's hall of fame and in the Herald's Guide.

## How to play

- 📖 **The Herald's Guide.** Every player gets it on joining: a book that explains what to look for and keeps your diary, with the unique structures you have discovered and clues to the ones still missing. Ask for an up-to-date copy at any time with `/heraldia guide`.
- 📊 **What the server has found.** `/heraldia status` shows how many villages, unique structures, medium structures and mines the players have discovered, the guardians defeated, the arena record and the quests handed in.
- 🧭 **Your journal.** `/heraldia journal` shows in chat, kind by kind and with a progress bar, what you have found and what you are still missing: village families, unique structures, medium structures, mine sizes, lighthouses and shipwrecks, the Nether and the End. Click a kind to see the names. No coordinates: finding them is up to you.
- 📋 **Village quests.** Touch the job board lectern on a village square (or use `/heraldia quest` there): 2 to 4 quests show up in chat (bring items, defeat a guardian, explore, travel to a neighbouring village, the arena) and change every few days. They pay emeralds, experience and reputation with that village: as known, friend and village hero, its villagers lower their prices by 10, 20 and 30 %. Attacking its villagers or golems lowers it.
- 🐪 **Caravans.** Now and then a Heraldia merchant with its llamas (and sometimes a guard) travels the road between two neighbouring villages near you. If bandits ambush it, defend it: when it arrives it pays emeralds and reputation with its destination village. If the merchant dies, the caravan is lost.
- 🚶 **Wayfarers.** Life on the roads, also near you: a pilgrim on the way to a monastery or cathedral, a shepherd with its flock, a messenger in a hurry, a pair of guards on patrol or a hunter coming back with its dog, as fits the area. They walk a stretch and go; the shepherd and the hunter trade, and hurting them is known in their village.
- 💬 **Talk to the villagers.** Right-click a villager in a Heraldia village and it talks to you in chat: lines by its trade, its land, the hour, the weather and how well the village knows you, with buttons to keep talking, ask for news or trade (sneak + right click trades directly; vanilla villagers trade as always). One in ten has travelled and, if the village trusts you, tells you once which way and how far a structure lies.
- 🏆 **Advancements.** A Heraldia tab with 124 advancements: a village of each family, every unique structure with its guardian and its treasure, the mines, the lighthouses and wrecks, the medium structures, the job board, the arena and the caravans.
- 🗿 **Trophies.** Each of the 22 guardians drops its trophy, an item with a name and a story. Collect them all to complete the Hall of Trophies.
- 🗺️ **Treasure maps.** Each map marks the nearest unique structure of its kind. They turn up in the villages' epic chests and the cartographers sell them.
- 🧭 **Treasure hunt.** Now and then a chest holds a treasure map fragment. Use it and it becomes a map with a mark: each mark is a real Heraldia structure that hides the next clue, and the last one leads to a chest buried by Ysolda, with good loot and a keepsake.
- 📜 **The Chronicle of the Heralds.** Each unique structure keeps a leaf of the chronicle written by Ysolda, the last herald of Atlantis. Each leaf points to the next: gather all 22 and you will learn how it ends. Defeat every guardian of the main world and Ysolda leaves you her last leaf, the title of Herald of Atlantis and the Standard of the Heralds, a banner to raise wherever you like.

The texts of the mod are in 8 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Catalan and Galician.

## Installation

1. You need **Minecraft 26.3**, **Fabric Loader** 0.20.1 or later and **Fabric API**.
2. Put `heraldia-<version>.jar` in the `mods` folder of the **server** and restart it.
3. **Players do not need the mod**: they can join with a vanilla client. Texts the client does not know show in Spanish, except the Herald's Guide, quests, the arena, caravans and Heraldia's notices, which arrive in each player's language. If they install the mod as well, they see everything in their own language and get the configuration screen (with Mod Menu).

**An existing world** works fine: the new structures appear in chunks that have not been generated yet (explore new land). If your world was created with Heraldia **0.1.0**, set `unicas.rejilla_compatible` to `true` before you start the server, so that `/locate`, treasure maps and the Guide keep finding the unique structures you already have.

**Commands** are in English on every server: `/heraldia guide`, `/heraldia key`, `/heraldia arena place`... The old Spanish names (`/heraldia guia`...) still work for now: they do the same and tell you the new name.

**New versions:** the server checks once a day whether there is a new Heraldia version for its Minecraft and tells operators when they join, with a download link. **Back up the server before updating.** Turn it off with `update_check.activado`.

**Updating the server:** nothing already built changes when you update. To bring new content to land that is already generated, `/heraldia update analyze` shows (and changes nothing) what would be regenerated or placed, always keeping a 50-block margin around anything players built, and `/heraldia update apply` does it after a confirmation, with an optional backup and `/heraldia update undo`. Step by step: [Updating a server](docs/CONFIGURATION.md#updating-a-server).

**Web maps:** if the server has BlueMap, Dynmap or squaremap, Heraldia's structures appear on the web map, each kind on its own layer (unique structures, villages, medium structures, mines, lighthouses and wrecks and the arena), only once a player has discovered them. Operators update it with `/heraldia webmap refresh`.

## Most useful options

The file `config/heraldia.json` is created on the server's first start, with a help text for every option. Changes apply when the server is restarted, in newly generated chunks. Options that do not affect generation (difficulty, bosses, loot, arena, Guide...) can be applied without restarting with `/heraldia reload`. Operators can also change the most useful ones in game, without the mod on their client, with `/heraldia config` (a menu with the profile and seven sections). Each player can choose the language of Heraldia's texts with `/heraldia language`.

| Option | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `perfil` | `estandar` | Gameplay profile: `casual`, `estandar`, `hardcore`, `ligero` (modest machines: lighter world generation; best in a new world) or `personalizado`; options written in the file override it. |
| `aldeas.separacion_bloques` | 1500 | Average distance between villages (1000-10000). |
| `aldeas.maximo` | 100 | Maximum number of villages in the world. |
| `aldeas.caminos.activado` | `true` | Roads between villages. |
| `unicas.<id>.activada` | `true` | Turns a unique structure on or off. |
| `unicas.rejilla_compatible` | `false` | `true` only for worlds created with Heraldia 0.1.0. |
| `medianas.frecuencia_porcentaje` | 100 | How many medium structures (200 = twice as many). |
| `faros.activado`, `naufragios.activado` | `true` | Lighthouses and wrecked galleons on the coast. |
| `dificultad.nivel` | `normal` | Guardians and monsters: `facil`, `normal`, `dificil` or `heroica`. |
| `botin.reponer.activado` | `false` | Heraldia chests are restocked after a few days. |
| `arena.activado` | `true` | The wave arena near spawn. |
| `arena.temporada.dia_reinicio` | `lunes` | Day the arena's weekly leaderboard closes and its champions are announced (at midnight, server time). |
| `encargos.activado` | `true` | Job board quests (`/heraldia quest`). |
| `reputacion.activado` | `true` | Reputation per village and its discounts. |
| `reputacion.aldea_visible` | `true` | The village shows its best reputation with vanilla blocks: flowers, banners, lanterns (or shutters closed); never on a player's block. |
| `dialogos.activado` | `true` | Conversations with Heraldia villagers (sneak + right click trades directly). |
| `caravanas.activado` | `true` | Caravans along the roads (operators: `/heraldia caravan`). |
| `viajeros.activado` | `true` | Wayfarers along the roads, each kind with its own option (operators: `/heraldia caravan wayfarer`). |
| `chozas_mago.prob_malvado` | 20 | Chance (%) that the wizard of a wizard's hut is an evil wizard in disguise. |
| `ambiente.activado` | `true` | Ambient effects with the game's own sounds: the village bell at sunrise and nightfall, the lighthouse siren in the rain and drips in the mines (each also has its own option). |
| `guia.entregar_al_entrar` | `true` | Gives the Herald's Guide to each player on joining. |
| `update_check.activado` | `true` | Tells operators when there is a new version. |
| `actualizar.margen_bloques` | 50 | Safety margin around player builds for `/heraldia update`. |
| `mapas_web.solo_descubiertas` | `true` | Only structures already discovered appear on the web map. |
| `tesoro.probabilidad` | 3.0 | Chance (%) of a treasure map fragment in each chest of medium structures and mines. |
