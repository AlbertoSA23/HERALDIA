Heraldia: a world worth exploring

Heraldia is a Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 dedicated entirely to world generation. It adds enormous, hand-designed villages in 36 biome styles, 14 unique structures packed with secrets, traps, puzzles and bosses, and a single sunken city of Atlantis per world.

It adds no new blocks, items or mobs: everything is built with vanilla blocks, items and loot, so it plays like the base game, only with a far richer world to discover. Perfect for survival, adventure and exploration playthroughs.

At a glance

  • 72 huge villages: 36 styles (3 per biome family), each in a standard and a large size.
  • 14 unique structures: castle, cathedral, pyramid temple, wizard's tower, haunted mansion and more, each with its own guardian boss.
  • Atlantis: a walled city of about 770 × 770 blocks, fully submerged, with a final guardian and legendary tridents.
  • Treasure maps for every unique structure, found in chests or sold by cartographers.
  • Fully configurable through config/heraldia.json.
  • Server-friendly: pure worldgen, no extra content to sync beyond the mod itself.

Huge villages

Forget the tiny vanilla hamlets. Heraldia villages are planned towns that follow the character of their biome. By default there is one village every 1,000-2,000 blocks, at most 100 per world (the ones closest to the spawn), and never within 10 chunks of a vanilla village. The standard version appears three times as often as the large one.

Every village includes:

  • A main square with a fountain, benches, trees, a bell and two golems.
  • Four avenues with special buildings: a church or temple fitting the style (with cemetery and crypt), town hall, market, tavern, smithy, library, farm with windmill and inn (or a port on the coast).
  • About 20 houses (standard) or 30 (large), fully furnished, with chests holding loot from common to epic.
  • Watchtowers and wall gates at the ends of the streets, and themed decoration between houses.
  • Villagers: half as many as houses, over 80% with a profession, protected by iron golems.

Unique structures

One every 3,000-5,000 blocks on average, each restricted to its own biomes. They are dungeons in the best sense: hidden rooms behind secret doors, redstone puzzles, traps, spawners, a guardian boss and a well-hidden treasure chamber.

  • Castle (plains, sunflower plains, meadows): a 100 × 100 fortress with a moat, curtain wall, barbican, keep and a dungeon with cells. Guardian: Sir Aldric. 36 chests.
  • Gothic cathedral (forests, flower forests): twin towers, rose window, flying buttresses, cloister and a crypt with ossuaries. Guardian: Friar Osmundo. 19 chests.
  • Pyramid temple (jungles): a stepped Mesoamerican pyramid with a ball court, an inner cenote and the tomb of the Jaguar King behind a secret door. 13 chests.
  • Wizard's tower (dark forest, pale garden): eight storeys with a library, laboratory, greenhouse and observatory, plus an experiments basement. Guardian: Archmage Malvarius. 16 chests.
  • Haunted mansion (swamps): a Victorian estate with a cemetery, mausoleum and a basement vault locked by a lever combination. Guardian: Count Malachias. 33 chests.
  • Eastern palace (cherry groves, bamboo jungles): a walled compound with three courtyards, throne room, library, treasury, a five-storey pagoda and secret piston doors.
  • Ice fortress (ice spikes, snowy plains and taiga): ice walls and towers over crypts. Guardian: the Frost King. 11 chests.
  • Dwarven citadel (stony and jagged peaks): a city carved into a mountain with forges, mines, a bridge over a chasm and two redstone puzzles. Guardian: Borin Ironbeard.
  • Lighthouse with smugglers' cave (stony shore, beaches): a lighthouse on a headland and a secret passage to a sea-level cave with a dock, dungeon and the captain's treasure.
  • Ruined coliseum (savanna): an elliptical amphitheater with a hypogeum, beast cells, an armory and redstone puzzles. Guardian: the Arena Champion.
  • Mountain monastery (meadows, groves): terraces on a crag, a church, a cloister and caves with puzzles and traps.
  • Observatory (windswept hills, windswept savanna): a copper dome with telescope and planetarium, and an archive underneath with a moon-phase puzzle. Guardian: Keeper of the Stars.
  • Ruined aqueduct (desert, badlands): a two-tier aqueduct, an abandoned Roman city, a cistern and sewers hiding the treasure.
  • Stranded galleon (beaches): a three-masted galleon run aground, with a cannon deck, a castaway camp and a cave with a lantern puzzle. Guardian: The Drowned Captain. 18 chests.

Atlantis

Exactly one per world, always. A walled city of about 770 × 770 blocks, completely underwater, in the deep-ocean basin closest to the origin that is at least 1,500 blocks away and unexplored.

  • Flooded streets and squares, and more than 200 dry buildings that you enter through doors or by diving up from below. Break a wall and the room floods.
  • Eight districts: agora, fish market, baths, arsenal, sunken harbor, coral gardens, academy and amphitheater, with a temple in the center and underground galleries.
  • Defenders: guardians, tridented drowned, 48 spawners, elder guardians in the key districts and the Guardian of Poseidon at the bottom.
  • Three seals with their keys, combination doors, arrow traps, water and TNT.
  • 185 chests with advanced loot, plus a hidden unique chest locked with the final guardian's key, holding two legendary tridents. First come, first served.

Find it with /locate structure heraldia:unicas/atlantida or with its treasure map.

Treasure maps

Every unique structure has its own map, marked with an X and carrying the structure's translated name.

  • 5% of the epic chests in Heraldia villages hold one.
  • Master cartographers (level 5) in Heraldia villages sell them for 24 emeralds and a compass.
  • The search covers about 10,000 blocks and never loads explored chunks, so it won't stall your server.

Installation

Requirements: Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or later, and Fabric API (tested with 0.166.2+26.3).

  1. Download the .jar and drop it in the mods folder of the server and every client.
  2. Launch the game. The log shows Heraldia: 87 estructuras registradas.

Important: in an existing world, structures only appear in chunks that have not been generated yet. Explore new territory to find them. World generation is not reloaded with /reload; restart the server.

Useful commands

  • /locate structure heraldia:llanuras_castellana: find the nearest village of a style.
  • /place structure heraldia:llanuras_castellana: place a village where you stand (add _grande for the large one).
  • /locate structure heraldia:unicas/atlantida: find Atlantis.
  • /function heraldia:muestrario: a guided tour of all 72 villages (use only in a test world).

Configuration

The file config/heraldia.json is created on first launch with every default value and an in-file help text. It is read at server start and affects chunks generated afterwards. Highlights:

  • Village spacing, maximum per world, share of large villages and distance to vanilla villages.
  • Enable or disable whole biome families or individual styles.
  • Enable, disable or re-space each unique structure, and set the minimum distance between different unique structures.
  • Automatic natural-golem cleanup in villages (on by default) with adjustable limits.

Compatibility and notes

  • Vanilla content only. No assets from other mods.
  • Compatibility with structure mods from other authors has not been verified.
  • Known limitations: ports are laid out without searching for water, and on steep slopes you may see steps and tall foundations.
